Aster

A PostgreSQL query-plan ranking prototype with an explicit uncertainty-aware fallback. The current version is intentionally transparent: it establishes a real executable ranking contract before any learned model or benchmark claim is allowed onto the portfolio.

Lower score is better. Aster falls back to PostgreSQL's default when the best candidate has uncertainty > 0.35 or when the score margin over the runner-up is < 0.08. These are prototype policy values, not production-tuned thresholds.
