Campus software has to earn the right to connect people.

A student platform for finding study buddies and study spots. It combines verified Waseda access, private messaging, reporting tools, and AI-assisted matching.

Next.js, TypeScript, Firebase. The public repository contains its authentication and matching implementation, deterministic tests, and AI evaluation harness.
