ENGINEERING / 01

Matching that respects the user.

The frontend requests verified recommendations from the FastAPI backend. Major, course and availability overlap establish a candidate pool. AI Study Match extracts intent server-side and ranks the existing profiles without sending their records to Gemini.

ENGINEERING / 02

Privacy is a feature.

Private pages require authenticated users. The backend redacts Instagram information until a study request is accepted. Messaging, reporting and study-spot discovery operate within the authenticated application.
